# squarebuzz privacy policy

**Effective date:** 8 October 2026

squarebuzz is a picture-logic puzzle game (nonograms) for children aged 6 and up, with no upper
age limit. Adults can enjoy it just as much. It runs on
Android, iPhone and iPad, and Windows. This policy explains what the app does with information.
In short: **squarebuzz does not collect, send or share any personal information.**

## What the app keeps, and where

Everything squarebuzz remembers stays **on your device**, inside the app's own storage:

- **Game progress**: campaign levels finished, stars, the daily-play streak, which pictures have
  been found, trophies, and the dates these happened.
- **Games in progress**, so a puzzle can be continued later.
- **Settings**: sound, music, theme, language, controls, game helpers, and the screen-time
  reminder chosen in the grown-ups' area.
- **A small error log**, written only if saving or loading fails. It records the time, which
  operation failed and the technical error details. It never contains puzzle content, settings
  or anything typed into the app.

The app never asks for a name, email address, age, photo, location or contacts. It has no
accounts and no sign-in.

## What the app does not do

- It does not connect to the internet. On Android the app does not even request the internet
  permission.
- It shows no advertising and contains no in-app purchases.
- It includes no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs that
  collect data.
- It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
- It does not share information with anyone, because it has nothing to share.

## Voice narration

When voice narration is switched on, the app reads text aloud using the text-to-speech engine
built into your device's operating system. The text is handed to that engine on the device;
squarebuzz does not send it anywhere.

## Links out of the app

The only links that leave the app, such as this privacy policy, sit behind a grown-ups' check
(a multiplication question) so that a child cannot follow them alone. Once you leave the app,
the website or store you open has its own privacy policy.

## Device backups

Your device's operating system may include app data in its own backups, for example Android's
backup to your Google account or an iCloud backup on iPhone and iPad. Those backups are made and
stored by the operating system provider under their own privacy policies, not by squarebuzz.

## Children's privacy

squarebuzz is designed with children aged 6 and up in mind and welcomes adult players too.
Because it collects no personal information from anyone,
there is nothing to collect from a child, and nothing for a parent to request, review or delete
beyond what is stored on the device.

## Removing the data

- **Reset progress** in *Options → Parent zone* erases game progress on the device.
- **Uninstalling the app** removes everything it stored.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with information, this policy will be updated before that
version is released, and the effective date above will change. The history of every change is
public in this repository.
